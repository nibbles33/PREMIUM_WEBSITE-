## 4. Per-route research / source summary

### Salon / Barber
- **Treatment vs. premises liability** — distinct claim types [COVERAGE]
- **Higher-risk services** (laser, injections, medical aesthetics) require disclosure — not automatic [UNDERWRITING]
- **Booth renters** often independent — not auto-covered under owner policy [UNDERWRITING]
- **Ontario personal service settings** — public health may apply depending on services [REGULATORY] — hedged, no fabricated licensing

### Non-Profit
- **ONCA governance duties** — directors have statutory duties; D&O does not eliminate them [REGULATORY]
- **Volunteer vs. WSIB** — regulatory question separate from optional volunteer accident [REGULATORY] vs [COVERAGE]
- **Abuse/molestation** — conditional on programs serving vulnerable persons [COVERAGE]
- **Funder certificates** — contractual [UNDERWRITING]

### Warehousing
- **Owned property vs. customers' goods** — critical distinction [COVERAGE]
- **Warehouse legal liability / bailee** — policy and contract dependent [COVERAGE]
- **3PL contract limits** — contractual [UNDERWRITING]
- **Commodity/hazmat/cold storage** — underwriting disclosure [UNDERWRITING]

### Property Management
- **Manager GL/E&O ≠ building insurance** — owner/corporation carries structure [COVERAGE] boundary
- **CMSA / CMRAO** — condominium management licensing; not all property managers [REGULATORY]
- **Management agreement certificates** — contractual [UNDERWRITING]
- **RECO brokerage** — not conflated with property management without evidence
